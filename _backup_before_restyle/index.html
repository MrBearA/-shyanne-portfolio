About me

Hello, I'm Shyanne. I'm a passionate game developer with a strong focus on 3D art. My expertise includes character modelling, environment design and creating optimised, game-ready assets.

I'm always improving my skills in 3D workflows, texturing and asset integration, and I enjoy collaborating with others to create immersive and engaging game experiences. I aspire to become a senior 3D artist at a game development studio.
